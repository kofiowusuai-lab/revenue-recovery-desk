Revenue Recovery Desk Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 21, 2026 · Last updated: June 21, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Revenue Recovery Desk service (the “Service”), operated by FlowAudit (“FlowAudit”, “we”, “us”), collects, uses, shares, and protects information when businesses use our website, onboarding forms, secure integration flows, recovery workflows, secrets vault, reports, and related services at flowaudit.co.uk/revenue-recovery and revenue-recovery-web-ivory.vercel.app.

Plain-English summary: we collect what we need to assess overdue revenue, connect to the systems you approve, run recovery outreach under your rules, keep audit records, and support you. Secrets are never sent by chat or email, you submit API keys through a one-time, browser-encrypted vault link, and you connect CRMs through revocable OAuth. You can ask us to access, export, or delete your business data at any time.

1. Who We Are (Data Controller / Processor)

For your own business and account information (the data you give us to set up and run your account), FlowAudit acts as a data controller. For your customers’/debtors’ personal data that we process to perform recovery on your behalf, FlowAudit acts as a data processor and you are the controller. You are responsible for having a lawful basis to share that customer data with us for recovery purposes.

2. Information We Collect

  • Business and contact information: company name, website, industry, contact names, email addresses, phone numbers, roles, and onboarding answers.
  • Revenue recovery configuration: invoice volumes, overdue balances, payment terms, customer segments, recovery rules, escalation preferences, tone/voice and SOP, business hours, discount limits, and do-not-contact instructions.
  • Integration credentials and tokens: API keys and OAuth tokens for the systems you approve (payment, accounting, CRM, email, messaging, letters). See “How we handle secrets” below.
  • Customer/debtor data: data pulled from the systems you authorize, which may include customer names, business contact details, invoice numbers, balances, due dates, statuses, notes, and payment links.
  • Communications: recovery messages drafted and sent, replies received, support emails, and approval decisions.
  • Usage, device, and log data: form submissions, timestamps, IP-derived technical data, audit logs, error logs, and security events.

3. How We Handle Secrets (Vault & OAuth)

  • Secrets vault (zero-knowledge): when we need an API key (e.g. Stripe), you receive a one-time link that encrypts the value in your browser using a public key. Only the private key held on our operator system can decrypt it; the private key is never uploaded to our servers. The link expires and burns after a single deposit.
  • OAuth connections: for CRMs, accounting/payment platforms, and business email/file systems (e.g. Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho, Xero, QuickBooks, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365/Outlook, Pipedrive) we request the minimum, read-oriented scopes needed for recovery. Tokens are stored encrypted and used only for the recovery purpose. You can revoke access at any time from within your provider’s settings.
  • Never by email/chat: please do not send passwords, API keys, or payment credentials by email, SMS, or chat. Use only the secure vault or OAuth flows we provide.

Google API Services: Limited Use

FlowAudit Revenue Recovery Desk’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

When you connect Google Workspace, we request only read-only scopes:

  • gmail.readonly (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.readonly): read-only access to your Gmail messages so the recovery agent can identify invoice, payment, and overdue-account correspondence relevant to collecting what you are owed. We do not send, modify, label, or delete any email.
  • drive.metadata.readonly (https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.metadata.readonly): read-only access to Google Drive file metadata (such as file names) to locate relevant billing or invoice documents. We do not read Drive file contents and cannot change or delete files.

Consistent with Limited Use, data obtained through these Google scopes is used only to provide and improve the user-facing revenue-recovery features you request. We do not use it for advertising, we do not sell it, and we do not transfer it to others except as needed to provide the Service, to comply with applicable law, or as part of a merger or acquisition. No humans read this Google user data except where you give explicit consent, where required for security or to comply with applicable law, or where the data has been aggregated and anonymized. You can revoke this access at any time from your Google Account’s connected-apps settings.

4. How We Use Information

  • To provide, operate, secure, and improve the Service.
  • To evaluate overdue revenue and prioritize recovery workflows.
  • To generate drafts, reports, payment-link workflows, reminders, and approved outreach in your voice.
  • To enforce your configured guardrails, approval gates, do-not-contact rules, sending hours, discount caps, and audit trails.
  • To troubleshoot integrations, prevent abuse and fraud, comply with legal obligations, and communicate with you.

6. Per-Client Isolation

Each signed client’s recovery agent runs in an isolated environment with credentials scoped to that client. We design the architecture so one client’s secrets and data are not exposed to another client.

7. AI-Assisted Processing

We use AI-assisted tools to classify invoices, summarize records, draft communications, and suggest next actions. We apply operational guardrails and human-approval controls according to your configuration. You should review AI-generated outputs before relying on them where approval is required or where legal/compliance sensitivity exists.

8. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards, including zero-knowledge secret handoff, encryption of stored tokens, least-privilege access, audit logging, and credential destruction on offboarding. No internet service can guarantee perfect security.

9. Data Retention

We keep information for as long as needed to provide the Service and then for legal, accounting, audit, and dispute-resolution purposes. Business and financial records may be retained for up to six (6) years after offboarding to meet UK statutory record-keeping obligations. On verified offboarding, we complete the work, take down active integrations, and destroy live credentials; remaining records are archived with a retention countdown and then purged.

10. Your Rights

Subject to applicable law (including the UK GDPR and Data Protection Act 2018), you may request access, correction, deletion, export/portability, or restriction of your business information by contacting us. Requests involving end-customer personal data are routed through the client business that controls that data. You also have the right to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). We may retain information where required for security, legal, audit, billing, or dispute-resolution purposes.

11. International Transfers

Information may be processed in countries other than where it was collected, depending on the systems and vendors used. Where required, appropriate safeguards (such as standard contractual clauses) are applied, and you remain responsible for confirming that your use of the Service and connected systems is lawful for your jurisdictions and customer base.

12. Children

The Service is intended for businesses and is not directed to children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

13. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updated policies are posted on this page with a new effective date. Continued use of the Service after changes means the updated policy applies.

14. Contact
