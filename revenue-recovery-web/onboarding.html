      Sending setup
      Letters / direct mail (only if you tick "Letter" above)
      Connect your PostGrid account (for posting letters):
      1. Create a free account at postgrid.com.
      2. Verify your sender / return address.
      3. Open Settings → API Keys and copy your Live API key.
      4. We'll email you a secure one-time link to connect it. Never paste the key into this form — letters post on your own PostGrid account, billed to you, on your letterhead.

      Letter billing notice: if you keep Letters enabled but do not provide your own PostGrid API key, you can opt out in the secure access form. If you do not opt out and later approve/authorize physical letters, any postage, print, processing, certified-mail, or related letter costs we incur will be passed through and billed at month-end in addition to your maintenance/retainer fee. Current per-piece estimate: $1.219 per letter + 20p per page (plus any certified-mail or provider pass-through extras).
