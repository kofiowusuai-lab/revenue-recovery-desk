What this process allows us to do

Use the staged recovery cadence below for overdue accounts, while stopping immediately for disputes, hardship signals, stop requests, or anything needing a human decision.

Pre-flight: verify customer, invoice/reference, balance, due date, payment route, recent replies, disputes, partial payments, and do-not-contact flags before any outreach.
Stage 1 — Friendly reminder: helpful first reminder with amount, due date, and secure payment route where available.
Stage 2 — Follow-up: clear follow-up asking for payment or a confirmed payment date.
Stage 3 — Firm notice: professional, direct notice that the account remains overdue.
Stage 4 — Pre-escalation: firm warning that the account may be escalated internally. Human approval required unless pre-authorized.
Stage 5 — Final notice / formal demand: formal factual notice. Always human-approved; physical/certified letters are approval-gated and cost-gated.
Stage 6 — Handback: stop automated recovery and package audit trail for client decision if legal/collections/write-off is needed.

Non-negotiable guardrails

  • No customer contact without your authorized channels and guardrails.
  • Every message must reference a real overdue invoice or balance from the connected source of truth.
  • We stop and escalate when a customer replies, disputes, asks to stop, says they paid, or needs a human.
  • No threats, legal claims, discounts, fees, or term changes unless explicitly authorized.
  • Every draft/send/reply/dispute/payment signal is logged.
