# Solicitor Review Checklist and Research Notes

**Not legal advice. Use this to brief a qualified solicitor.**

## Highest-priority review questions
1. **Contracting entity:** Interim decision is founder/sole trader trading as FlowAudit. Solicitor should confirm exact legal-name wording, address/trading disclosures, tax/VAT handling, and personal-liability implications. Avoid an undefined “FlowAudit” brand signature.
2. **Pre-incorporation risk:** Companies Act 2006 s51 can make the person signing personally liable for a company that does not yet exist.
3. **Regulated/consumer debt boundary:** Keep initial service B2B commercial invoice recovery only unless counsel confirms consumer/regulated-credit scope.
4. **Client-money boundary:** Payments should go direct to client/Stripe/etc. FlowAudit should not hold client money without legal/regulatory advice.
5. **Authority wording:** Make FlowAudit a limited operational agent, not a debt purchaser, solicitor, bailiff/enforcement agent, credit bureau, or legal representative.
6. **DPA:** Confirm UK GDPR controller/processor split and Article 28 clauses.
7. **Liability caps:** Confirm caps are reasonable under UCTA for B2B standard terms.
8. **Success-fee definition:** If used, define recovered revenue and attribution precisely.
9. **AI disclaimers and approval gates:** Ensure clients cannot blame FlowAudit for unapproved business/legal decisions while still making the service fair.
10. **Offboarding/retention:** Align six-year retention with statutory/accounting/dispute needs and privacy notices.

## Contract pack gaps to fill before first signature
- Founder full legal name, trading address/principal place of business, and any required trading disclosures.
- VAT status.
- Final pricing model and whether success fees apply.
- Exact retainer wording and payment interval.
- Approved sender identity and email deliverability responsibilities.
- Whether letters, SMS/WhatsApp or phone calls are in scope.
- Whether any client base includes consumers, sole traders, partnerships or guarantors.
- Whether FlowAudit will ever create payment links or only use existing hosted invoice URLs.
- Final liability cap amount and whether a separate data/security cap is needed.
- Whether assignment to a future company should require client consent or be automatic with notice.
