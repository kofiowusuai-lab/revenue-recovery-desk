## 4. Approved message tone
Tone: professional, factual, calm, non-threatening, no shame language.  
Do not imply legal action, credit damage, service suspension or personal consequences unless the Client has separately approved exact wording.

## 5. Approval rules
Require approval before:
- first live send for a client;
- any new template;
- any message above the amount threshold;
- any account with dispute/complaint/legal sensitivity;
- any discount, settlement, write-off or payment plan outside the limits;
- any formal demand, legal referral, letter before action or solicitor involvement;
- any non-email channel unless pre-approved.

Write-off authority: none unless explicitly approved.

## 7. Disputes and complaints
If a debtor/customer disputes the invoice, complains, requests no contact, threatens legal action, reports insolvency, or indicates vulnerability/consumer status, the Provider must pause recovery and escalate to the Client.

## 8. Data and system permissions
The Client authorises the Provider to access the connected systems listed in the Order Form solely to provide the Services.

The Provider may not:
- change invoice amounts;
- issue refunds;
- delete customer records;
- create new charges or payment links unless specifically authorised;
- modify contracts or payment terms;
- access unrelated systems or data.

## 9. Audit and reporting
The Provider will maintain operational records of accounts identified, drafts created, approvals requested/received, messages sent, outcomes/resolution status, and exceptions/escalations.

## 10. Stop conditions
The Provider must stop or pause workflows if the Client withdraws authority; credentials are suspected compromised; data appears inaccurate; contact appears unlawful or unsafe; the account is disputed or legally sensitive; or the Provider determines activity may breach law, platform terms or this schedule.
